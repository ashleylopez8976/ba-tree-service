About Us

BA Tree Service, LLC is a family-owned business led by brothers Bryson and Anthony Lopez. Together, we bring more than 18 years of combined professional arborist experience. We approach every project with a strong work ethic, attention to detail, and respect for our customers and their property.

Our mission is to become Oklahoma’s most trusted tree care company by providing high-quality workmanship, outstanding customer service, safe operations, and reliable solutions that exceed customer expectations.
